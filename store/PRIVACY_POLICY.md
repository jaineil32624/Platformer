# Privacy Policy — Lumen Leap

_Last updated: October 19, 2026_

Lumen Leap ("the game") is developed by Jaineil Rana ("we").

## Summary

**We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.** The game has no accounts, no ads,
no analytics and no tracking.

## Data stored on your device

The game saves the following **only on your own device** (browser local storage or the app's private
storage) so you can continue playing:

- game progress, scores, achievements and statistics for up to three save slots;
- your settings (volume, controls, display options);
- an optional player name you type for the on-device leaderboard.

This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time with *Settings → Reset all progress*,
by clearing the site's data in your browser, or by uninstalling the app.

## Network access

The game does not send data over the internet. (The web version may be downloaded from a web host,
which may keep standard server logs under that host's own policy.)

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Permissions

The mobile apps request no special permissions. Vibration is used for gameplay feedback and can be
turned off in Settings.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
