six handcrafted levels — from a sunrise meadow to a storm-struck mountain peak.

• Run, sprint, double jump and wall jump with controls tuned to feel great

**A WORLD THAT CHANGES AS YOU PLAY**
• The sky shifts from dawn to dusk to midnight as you progress

**FRIENDS, FOES AND BOSSES**
• Stomp slimes, dodge spiky beetles, outsmart swooping bats
